# Data Processing Agreement

## Between:
- **Responsible Party**: Client (the data controller)
- **Operator**: C6 Group / RemotePay Fintech Services Pty Ltd (the data processor)

## 1. Scope of Processing
C6 Group processes personal data on behalf of the client for the purpose of providing AI services including:
- Content generation
- Trend analysis
- Market intelligence

## 2. Duration
This agreement remains in effect for the duration of the service contract.

## 3. Nature and Purpose
Processing of personal data as instructed by the client for legitimate business purposes.

## 4. Data Subject Categories
- Client employees
- Client customers (where applicable)

## 5. Security Measures
C6 Group implements:
- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security audits
- Employee confidentiality agreements

## 6. Sub-processors
C6 Group uses the following sub-processors:
- Local storage systems (on-premises)
- (No cloud providers at this time)

## 7. Data Subject Rights
C6 Group will assist the client in responding to data subject access requests within 20 business days.

## 8. Breach Notification
C6 Group will notify the client within 48 hours of discovering a data breach.

## 9. Data Return/Deletion
Upon termination, all personal data will be returned or deleted within 30 days.

## 10. Audit Rights
Client may request an annual security audit with 30 days' notice.
