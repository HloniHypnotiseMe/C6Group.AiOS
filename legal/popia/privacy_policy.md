# C6 Group / RemotePay Fintech Services Pty Ltd - Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how C6 Group ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

## 2. Information We Collect
- Contact information (name, email, phone)
- Business information (company name, role)
- Technical data (IP addresses, browser types)
- Communication records

## 3. Purpose of Processing
We process personal information for:
- Providing our AI services
- Communicating with clients
- Improving our systems
- Legal compliance

## 4. Lawful Basis
We process information based on:
- Consent from the data subject
- Necessity for contract performance
- Legal obligations
- Legitimate business interests

## 5. Data Subject Rights
Under POPIA, you have the right to:
- Request access to your information
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of information
- Object to processing
- Withdraw consent at any time

## 6. Data Security
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures including:
- Encryption of data in transit (TLS)
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security reviews
- Staff confidentiality agreements

## 7. Data Retention
Personal information is retained only as long as necessary for the purpose collected, or as required by law.

## 8. Cross-Border Data Transfer
We do not transfer personal information outside South Africa without appropriate safeguards or consent.

## 9. Breach Notification
In the event of a data breach, we will notify:
- The Information Regulator within 30 business days
- Affected data subjects where required
